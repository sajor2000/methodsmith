OUTCOME REPORTING extension for randomized trial protocols (SPIRIT-Outcomes 2022).
9-item extension covering outcome definition and justification, the target difference for sample size, instrument responsiveness, outcome assessors, and multiplicity in trial protocols. SPIRIT-Outcomes 2022 was written against SPIRIT 2013, which SPIRIT 2025 supersedes; apply it with the SPIRIT 2025 core, never instead of it. It is a matched pair with CONSORT-Outcomes 2022 for trial reports: when outcome reporting is reviewed, offer both.

**Primary reference:** Butcher NJ, Monsour A, Mew EJ, et al. Guidelines for Reporting Outcomes in Trial Protocols: The SPIRIT-Outcomes 2022 Extension. JAMA. 2022;328(23):2345-2356. doi:10.1001/jama.2022.21243. PMID: 36512367.

## Outcomes (items 12.1-12.5)

### Item 12.1 -- Primary outcome domain rationale

**Description:** Explain why the domain of the trial's primary outcome was selected.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Protocol or SAP text justifying the chosen primary outcome domain

### Item 12.2 -- Minimal important change

**Description:** When the primary outcome's analysis metric expresses within-participant change, define and justify the minimal important change for individuals.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Stated minimal important change value with its justification
- Change-score definition in the SAP matching it

### Item 12.3 -- Cutoff values for categorical analysis

**Description:** When the outcome data to be collected are continuous but will be analyzed as categorical (method of aggregation), state the cutoff values that will be used.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Planned categorization thresholds documented before analysis

### Item 12.4 -- Analysis time points

**Description:** When outcome assessments are planned at several post-randomization time points, name the time points that will be used for the analysis.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Analysis time points prespecified in the protocol or SAP

### Item 12.5 -- Composite outcome components

**Description:** When a composite outcome is used, define every individual component of the composite.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Component list in the protocol or outcome-definition table

## Sample size (item 14.1)

### Item 14.1 -- Target difference

**Description:** State and justify the target difference to be used between treatment groups (for example, the minimal important difference).

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Sample-size calculation inputs in the protocol matching the stated target difference
- Justification for the target difference value

## Data collection methods (items 18a.1-18a.2)

### Item 18a.1 -- Instrument responsiveness

**Description:** State what is known about how responsive the study instruments are in a population similar to the study sample.

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Responsiveness evidence cited for each outcome instrument
- Instrument choice justified for the target population

### Item 18a.2 -- Outcome assessors

**Description:** Name who will assess the primary outcome (for example, a nurse or parent).

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Assessor role named in the protocol; blinding of assessors stated where applicable

## Statistical methods (item 20a.1)

### Item 20a.1 -- Multiplicity

**Description:** Describe any planned methods for handling multiplicity in analyzing or interpreting the primary and secondary outcomes (for example, coprimary outcomes, one outcome assessed at several time points, or subgroup analyses of an outcome).

**What to look for in code/outputs:**
- Planned multiplicity adjustment in the protocol or SAP
- Adjustment scope matching the listed multiplicity sources
