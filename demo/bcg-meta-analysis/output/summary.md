BCG vaccine meta-analysis — committed demo outputs
==================================================

Data: metafor::dat.bcg (13 RCTs; public). Package: metafor 5.2.1, R 4.6.1.

Primary model: REML, Knapp-Hartung adjustment, prespecified.
Pooled log risk ratio -0.7145 (95% CI -1.1084 to -0.3206; HK-adjusted p = 0.00192; prediction interval -1.9960 to 0.5670); I2 = 92.2%, tau^2 = 0.3132, k = 13 trials.
Allocation-method subgroup (descriptive): test of moderators Q_M = 0.97 on 2 df, p = 0.413; random-effects slope for random allocation -0.4478 (95% CI -1.5470 to 0.6514).

Reading notes: the risk ratios below 1 favor BCG vaccination; the 1948-1955 trials
are heterogeneous, which the prediction interval carries honestly. This demo shows
the reporting lane (register, analyze, quote, display), not new science.
