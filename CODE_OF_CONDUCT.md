# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/),
adapted here for the Methodsmith community.

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation welcoming, safe, and equitable. Treat every
participant with dignity and respect, regardless of identity, background,
ability, language, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Engage kindly and honestly.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for contributions and repair harm when possible.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Credit sources and respect confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexualization,
unauthorized sharing of private information, threats or encouragement of harm,
impersonation, and other conduct that threatens community well-being are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a possible violation privately to the project owner through
[@sajor2000 on GitHub](https://github.com/sajor2000). Do not post private
details in a public issue. Project maintainers will review reports promptly
and confidentially, investigate as appropriate, and may issue a warning,
restrict participation, suspend access, or permanently ban a participant.
Enforcement will consider the impact and severity of the conduct and prioritize
participant safety.

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project.
